Chapter 02: AI Collaboration Modes

Understanding how humans can collaborate with AI in different ways

AI ke saath kaam karne ke 3 modes

There are three primary ways humans can work with AI systems:

  • Automation — “Do this task”
  • Augmentation — “Help me think”
  • Agency — “Pursue this goal for me”

Automation — “Do this task”

In automation mode, the AI performs a specific task with minimal human intervention. The human defines the task clearly and the AI executes it.

Examples:

  • Generating reports from data
  • Transcribing audio to text
  • Simple data entry and processing
  • Basic code generation for well-defined problems

Augmentation — “Help me think”

In augmentation mode, the AI works as a thinking partner, helping humans explore ideas, consider alternatives, and make better decisions.

Examples:

  • Brainstorming ideas and solutions
  • Research assistance and literature review
  • Editing and improving written work
  • Providing different perspectives on a problem

Agency — “Pursue this goal for me”

In agency mode, the AI acts autonomously to pursue a goal defined by the human, making decisions and taking actions within defined boundaries.

Examples:

  • Managing investment portfolios within risk parameters
  • Optimizing supply chain logistics
  • Running marketing campaigns with performance goals
  • Monitoring systems for anomalies and responding appropriately

Automation vs Augmentation vs Agency

Understanding when to use each mode is crucial for effective AI collaboration:

Use Automation when:

  • The task is well-defined and repetitive
  • Clear success criteria exist
  • Human judgment is not required for each step
  • Speed and consistency are priorities

Use Augmentation when:

  • Creative thinking or exploration is needed
  • Multiple perspectives would be valuable
  • You want to challenge your own assumptions
  • Learning and skill development are goals

Use Agency when:

  • The goal is clear but the path to achieve it is complex
  • Continuous monitoring and adjustment is required
  • You trust the AI to make decisions within boundaries
  • Scalability and ongoing operation are needed

AI autonomy ka concept

AI autonomy refers to the degree to which an AI system can operate independently:

  1. Level 0: No Automation — Human performs all tasks
  2. Level 1: Driver Assistance — AI provides warnings or momentary assistance
  3. Level 2: Partial Automation — AI controls some functions but human must monitor
  4. Level 3: Conditional Automation — AI drives under certain conditions but human must be ready to take over
  5. Level 4: High Automation — AI performs all driving functions under certain conditions
  6. Level 5: Full Automation — AI performs all driving functions under all conditions

In knowledge work, similar levels apply where higher levels indicate greater AI independence but always with human oversight and responsibility.

Kab kaunsa mode use karna hai?

Choosing the right mode depends on several factors:

•
Task clarity: Well-defined tasks favor automation; ambiguous tasks benefit from augmentation.
•
Risk tolerance: High-risk situations may require more human oversight (less agency).
•
Available time: When time is limited, automation or agency may be preferable.
•
Skill development: When learning is a goal, augmentation provides valuable practice.

Quick Revision

Three Collaboration Modes

Automation (AI does), Augmentation (AI helps think), Agency (AI pursues goals)

When to Use Each Mode

Automation: repetitive tasks; Augmentation: creative thinking; Agency: goal pursuit with oversight

AI Autonomy Levels

Ranges from no automation (Level 0) to full automation (Level 5) with human oversight
