Quick Revision - Key Concepts

AI Fluency

The human ability to effectively guide, judge, and collaborate with AI models.

The 4Ds Framework

Delegate → Describe → Discern → Diligence (Decide → Explain → Check → Own)

AI Collaboration Modes

Automation (AI does task), Augmentation (AI helps think), Agency (AI pursues goal)
