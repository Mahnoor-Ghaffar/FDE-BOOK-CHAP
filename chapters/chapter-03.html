Chapter 03: AI-Assisted Decision Making

Using AI to enhance human decision-making while maintaining oversight

Delegation → kya AI kare?

The first step in AI-assisted decision making is deciding what aspects of the decision process should be handled by AI versus what should remain with humans.

Key Considerations:

  • Problem definition and goal setting
  • Data requirements and quality
  • Ethical implications and bias considerations
  • Regulatory compliance requirements
  • Human expertise and judgment needs

Description → AI ko kya information dein?

Once the division of labor is decided, humans must provide AI with the information it needs to perform its assigned tasks effectively.

Product Description

WHAT chahiye? (Output type, Audience, Format, Length, Tone, Topics, Exclusions)

Process Description

HOW kaam karna hai? (Steps, Order, Method, Examples, Checks)

Performance Description

AI ko KAISE behave karna hai? (Concise vs detailed, Supportive vs challenging, etc.)

Discernment → result sahi hai?

After AI produces output, humans must evaluate its quality and trustworthiness before proceeding.

Discernment Dimensions:

  • Product discernment: Is the result correct, complete, consistent?
  • Process discernment: Is this way of working with AI actually paying off?
  • Performance discernment: When AI acts autonomously, is it serving people well?

Diligence → kya responsibly use kar rahe hain?

The final step ensures responsible AI use by examining creation, transparency, and deployment aspects.

Creation Diligence

Is it okay to put this data into this tool? (privacy, confidentiality, consent)

Transparency Diligence

Should people know AI was involved? (disclosure requirements, stakeholder communication)

Deployment Diligence

Did you verify the work before it goes out? (testing, validation, human approval)

Key Questions for Each D

Delegate: What should AI do? Describe: What does AI need? Discern: Is result good? Diligence: Responsible use?

Human Responsibility

Final responsibility for AI-assisted work always remains with humans
