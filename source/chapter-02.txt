## 📚 AI Fluency: A Crash Course — Topics

### 1. AI Fluency ka Big Picture

* AI access ≠ AI fluency
* AI Fluency kya hai?
* Effective, Efficient, Ethical & Safe AI use
* **4Ds Framework**
  * Delegation
  * Description
  * Discernment
  * Diligence
* **Decide → Explain → Check → Own**

### 2. AI ke saath kaam karne ke 3 modes

* **Automation** — “Do this task”
* **Augmentation** — “Help me think”
* **Agency** — “Pursue this goal for me”
* Automation vs Augmentation vs Agency
* AI autonomy ka concept
* Kab kaunsa mode use karna hai?

### 3. Delegation — Decide who should do what

* Delegation kya hai?
* Problem Awareness
* Platform Awareness
* Task Delegation
* Human vs AI responsibilities
* Workflow design
* AI ko kya dena hai aur kya khud karna hai?
* Human judgment ki importance

### 4. Description — Give AI what it needs

Description ke **3 types**:

* **Product Description** — WHAT chahiye?
  * Output type
  * Audience
  * Format
  * Length
  * Tone
  * Topics
  * Exclusions

* **Process Description** — HOW kaam karna hai?
  * Steps
  * Order
  * Method
  * Examples
  * Checks

* **Performance Description** — AI ko KAISE behave karna hai?
  * Concise vs detailed
  * Supportive vs challenging
  * Exploratory vs decisive
  * Questions poochna vs assumptions banana
  * Uncertainty flag karna

### 5. Discernment — AI ke output ko judge karna

* AI confident ho sakta hai lekin wrong bhi ho sakta hai
* AI output ki quality evaluate karna
* Correctness vs plausibility
* Evidence/checking
* AI ki limitations
* Kab AI par trust karna hai aur kab verify karna hai

### 6. Diligence — Responsible AI use

* AI ke output ki responsibility lena
* **Creation**
* **Transparency**
* **Deployment**
* Privacy & security
* Responsible AI use
* Human accountability

### 7. 4Ds ko ek real project mein combine karna

* Delegation → kya AI kare?
* Description → AI ko kya information dein?
* Discernment → result sahi hai?
* Diligence → kya responsibly use kar rahe hain?
* Complete AI workflow

### 8. AI Fluency → Agent Factory Engineering

* Personal AI skills se AI systems tak
* AI agents ke liye 4Ds ka importance
* Context engineering
* Agent boundaries
* Human escalation
* Digital FTEs
* System of Record
* AI governance

**Short mein chapter ka core formula:**

> **Delegation = Decide**
> **Description = Explain**
> **Discernment = Check**
> **Diligence = Own**
